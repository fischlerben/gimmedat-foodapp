Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 8, 2025

1. Introduction

GimmeDat ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and password
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party payment processors)
  • Order history and preferences
  • Location data (when you grant permission)
  • Referral codes and usage

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Process payments and manage your account
  • Send you order confirmations and updates
  • Provide customer support
  • Send promotional communications (with your consent)
  • Improve our services and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to store and process your data:

  • Firebase / Firestore – User data storage, order history, account information, and app functionality
  • Stripe – Payment processing for credit card and Apple Pay transactions
  • OpenNode – Bitcoin payment processing and cryptocurrency transactions

These third-party services have their own privacy policies and security measures. We encourage you to review their privacy practices.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and comply with legal obligations. Specifically:

  • Account information: Retained while your account is active
  • Order history: Retained for tax and accounting purposes as required by law
  • Payment information: Processed and stored by our payment processors according to their policies

You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. However, we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Help / Contact Us section in the app.
